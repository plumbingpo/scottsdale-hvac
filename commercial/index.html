Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Scottsdale HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the heat fast. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable in the meantime.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

Preventive Care

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment is preventing failures before they happen. Scottsdale HVAC offers customized scheduled maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes.
